Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pose Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1.Information We Collect

Media Files: Our app processes videos to perform pose estimation. Crucially, all video processing is done locally on your device. We do not upload your videos to any servers.

Device Information: We may collect information about your mobile device, including the device model, operating system version, and Android Advertising ID (GAID), collected through Google services.

Log and Crash Data: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect information about app crashes and performance. This helps us fix bugs and improve stability.

Usage Statistics: We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app (e.g., which features are used most often).

2.Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use personal data (such as advertising IDs) to show personalized ads.

You can control ad personalization at any time in your Google Account Ad Settings, or through your device's advertising settings.

3.Permissions

To function correctly, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage To select videos for processing and save the results to your gallery.
  • Internet Required for displaying advertisements, sending crash reports, and loading essential app components.

4.Data Retention

Your videos are processed entirely on-device and are never stored by us. Technical data is retained as follows:

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are retained for up to 90 days. Analytics data (Firebase Analytics) is retained for 14 months by default, in accordance with Google's standard retention policy. You can request earlier deletion by contacting us at the address below.

5.Data Security

We value your trust. Since your videos never leave your device, your personal media remains private. Technical data (crashes and analytics) is handled through secure Google infrastructure.

Your video content is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
